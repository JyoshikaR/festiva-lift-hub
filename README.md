 MAJOR PROBLEM :
 
 1. Unpredictable festival demand

During festivals, demand for products can suddenly increase, but businesses often cannot accurately estimate which products will sell more and by how much.

Solution:
Festiva analyzes normal sales versus festival-period sales and calculates Festival Lift %, helping businesses identify products with increased demand.

2. Inventory shortage and overstocking

Businesses may understock popular products, causing lost sales, or overstock products, causing unsold inventory after the festival.

Solution:
Our analytics dashboard highlights high-demand products, sales trends, and festival-wise demand patterns, helping businesses plan inventory better.

3. Customers struggle to find festival products

Festival-related products are often scattered across different stores and platforms, making it difficult for customers to find everything they need.

Solution:
Festiva provides a festival-focused marketplace where customers can discover products based on festivals and categories in one place.

4. Businesses lack festival-specific insights

Traditional sales reports may show total sales, but they don't clearly explain how much of the increase was caused by a particular festival.

Solution:
Festiva provides Festival Lift Analytics, showing:

Festival-wise sales
Product-wise performance
Normal vs festival sales
Demand trends
Festival Lift %
Top-performing products
5. Poor marketing decisions

Businesses may spend money promoting products without knowing which products are actually influenced by festival demand.

Solution:
Festiva identifies high-lift products and festival demand patterns, helping businesses make more informed promotional decisions.
